The demo index bundled with Rewind contains caption text from:

**MIT OpenCourseWare — 6.046J / 18.410J Introduction to Algorithms (SMA 5503), Fall 2005**
Prof. Charles Leiserson and Prof. Erik Demaine.
https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-046j-introduction-to-algorithms-sma-5503-fall-2005/

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

What was used: the WebVTT caption files that MIT OCW publishes on each lecture's resource page,
for Lectures 1–10. Exact caption URLs, YouTube IDs and per-item licences are listed in
`sources.json`. The captions were split into overlapping 45-second windows and embedded
locally. No audio or video was downloaded or redistributed. Result links open the original
lectures on YouTube.

Changes: text was segmented into windows and indexed; caption wording is unchanged apart from
whitespace and markup removal.

This demo index is shared under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence. It is used here for
